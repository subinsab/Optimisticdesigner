What I do
Four things.
All of them rare.

Most design leaders do one of these well. The interesting ones do all four.

01
I turn user frustration
into revenue.

Most products lose customers in the moments between intention and action. I find those moments, fix them, and measure the business impact. Aspero went from ₹18Cr in losses to ₹860M in revenue. That's not a design win — that's a business transformation.

₹860M revenue · ₹18Cr loss reversed · 18 months
02
I build organisations,
not just products.

Any designer can improve a screen. Very few can build the team, culture, and systems that improve every screen — consistently, at scale, without them in the room. I've done it twice. HashedIn went from 1 designer to 32. Yubi from 2 to 20. Both practices outlasted me.

1→32 at HashedIn · 2→20 at Yubi · Both acquired or scaled
03
I speak both languages —
design and business.

Design budgets don't get approved because they're good ideas. They get approved because someone translated them into a business case. I showed a CPTO a ₹136Cr invoice for design fragmentation. That conversation happened once. The Yubi Design System was built the next quarter.

₹136Cr saved · 44 designers unified · One meeting
04
I make AI work
for your team.

AI-assisted design isn't the future — it's table stakes for 2025. I pioneered Token Studio automation and AI-driven workflows at Yubi because a 20-person team serving 13 products cannot operate on manual handoffs. Bugs dropped from 139 to 4. Adoption hit 85%.

AI workflows · Token Studio · 68→12 FE engineers needed
