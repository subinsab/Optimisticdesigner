What I do

Four things. All of them rare.

Most design leaders do one of these well. The interesting ones do all four.

01

I build organisations, not just products.

Any designer can improve a screen. Very few can build the team, culture, and systems that improve every screen, consistently, at scale, without them in the room. I've done it twice. HashedIn went from 1 designer to 32. Yubi from 2 to a 20-plus org. Both practices outlasted me.

1 to 32 at HashedIn · 2 to 20+ at Yubi · Both acquired or scaled

02

I turn user frustration into revenue.

Most products lose customers in the moments between intention and action. I find those moments, fix them, and measure the business impact. Aspero went from ₹18Cr in losses to ₹860M in revenue. That's not a design win. That's a business transformation.

₹860M revenue · ₹18Cr loss reversed · 18 months

03

I speak both languages: design and business.

Design budgets don't get approved because they're good ideas. They get approved because someone translated them into a business case. I showed the CPTO the invoice for design fragmentation. That conversation happened once. Y.Design was built the next quarter, and operating costs fell by ₹72 crore in FY24 and ₹186 crore in FY25.

₹72Cr saved FY24 · ₹186Cr saved FY25 · One meeting

04

I make AI work for your team.

AI-assisted design isn't the future; it's table stakes. I pioneered Token Studio automation and AI-driven workflows at Yubi because a 20-person team serving 13 products cannot operate on manual handoffs. Bugs dropped from 139 to 4. Adoption hit 85%. Now I do it for other companies as a consultant, cutting design-to-development cycles by up to 40%.

AI workflows · Token Studio · Design-to-dev cycles down up to 40%

Philosophy
"Design should reward behaviour, not manipulate it. True delight lies in respect, not addiction."
01
Creativity needs structure. Systems are not cages. They're the rails that let creativity move at speed.
02
Design is a business function. Every decision connects to outcomes. Not a service layer; a growth lever.
03
Culture drives consistency. People create systems. Systems sustain creativity. Build the environment first.
04
Empathy builds leadership. Your team's clarity is your clarity. Listen first, always.
05
Profitability enables freedom. Operational efficiency is creative fuel. You can't experiment on an empty tank.
