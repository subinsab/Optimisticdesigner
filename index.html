Chapter One

Why I design

It started with a question, not a career plan. I kept wondering why some things feel effortless and others feel like work, and I couldn't stop pulling at that thread.

The thread kept leading somewhere bigger.

PixelsWhere curiosity started: making things look right.
ProductsThen making things work right, for real people.
SystemsThen making rightness repeatable at scale.
OrganizationsThen building the teams that build the systems.
LeadershipThen creating the conditions where all of it thrives.
OptimismAnd finally understanding what powered every step.
